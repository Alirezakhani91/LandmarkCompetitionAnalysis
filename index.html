How to read this index: Small ≤60 ft², Large >60–200 ft², Extra Large >200 ft². Regular base rate / square foot / 30 days = advertised 28-day rate ×30/28 ÷ area, or calendar-month rate ×12×30/365 ÷ area. Where an already normalized calendar-month equivalent is supplied, its conversion is consistent with the original billing period. We first calculate each unit's rate, then median within its facility and finally the median across facilities for each brand/city/size/type. A brand with many listings at one facility does not gain extra weight. Unknown features are not assumed to be Indoor, Outdoor or Climate Controlled. Review-only values are illustrative scenarios, never mixed into official medians. Tax, insurance, introductory promotions and unknown add-on charges are excluded from the base price; open unit details for source and observation date.
